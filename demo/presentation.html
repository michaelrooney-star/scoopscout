ScoopScout

Your daily scoop, planned around your city and calendar.

A free moment should feel like a break.

You have a gap between commitments, but finding a nearby ice cream shop, checking its hours, and figuring out whether you can get there and back takes time.

ScoopScout turns that gap into one practical outing. It combines your starting point, destination, preferences, and schedule with sourced shop facts.

Goal → find a gap → check shops → budget travel → choose a scoop

Open inference. Grounded planning.

Akamai + Qwen

Qwen/Qwen3-4B served by the workshop’s vLLM endpoint. Kubernetes port-forward connects the laptop to Akamai-hosted inference. An OpenAI-compatible call ranks eligible shops by preference.

Python + browser UI

Deterministic calendar-gap and travel-budget tools, transparent fallback if inference is unavailable, and a local HTML/CSS/JavaScript interface.

Calendar + location

Google Calendar FreeBusy OAuth is implemented. GPS or selected coordinates supply the starting point; an optional destination supports onward trips.

Privacy and evidence

Busy intervals and coordinates stay local. Nine sourced SF shops; fifteen passing tests. The demo uses a manual sample schedule. Live Google authorization still needs setup.
