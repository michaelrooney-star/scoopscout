Meet ScoopScout. It finds an ice cream outing that fits a gap in your day, from wherever you are in San Francisco.

Choose a starting point and where you need to end up. For this demo, we use Dolores Park and a manually entered sample schedule. Google Calendar is not connected yet.

The agent finds a thirty minute gap, checks published shop hours, and allows time for walking, enjoying a scoop, and a buffer before your next commitment.

Bi-Rite fits this gap. The estimated outing takes twenty-two minutes, leaving eight minutes before the next busy interval. Source and walking-route links help you verify the plan.

The decision summary explains the checks. Schedule and location stay in the local app. The Akamai-hosted Qwen model receives shop facts and preferences, without calendar times or coordinates.

Change the location to the Inner Sunset, and the recommendation changes to Hometown Creamery. The same calendar gap now has a shorter estimated outing.

The prototype includes Google Calendar authorization and five-minute checks while the page is open. Live calendar access still needs OAuth setup. Walking times and shop hours are estimates and snapshots, so check before leaving.
